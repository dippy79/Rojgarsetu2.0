A place where anyone can thrive

GitLab is the trusted enterprise platform for software creation in the AI era, used by more than 100,000 organizations to plan, build, secure, and deploy software at machine scale, guided by humans. Our mission is to unlock every team to ship software at the speed of imagination, and we're building the next chapter of GitLab to make that possible at the pace the agentic era demands.

We run our business on our own product, stay transparent in how we work, and create space for team members across the globe to show up as their full selves and do the best work of their careers. Learn more about our accommodations and accessibility.

Great Place to Work certified

At GitLab, being a great place to work means building a workplace where everyone can contribute and anyone can thrive. We aim to uphold that ideal in every country where we employ GitLab team members.
